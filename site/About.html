Inspections should be something a client can trust, not take your word for

CheckA+ was built by WheelsTurn, a South African automotive services group, to solve a problem we had ourselves: paper checklists that clients never saw, reports that took hours to put together, and no easy way to prove what was actually found on a vehicle.

We started with our own workshops, replacing paper job cards with a digital checklist that captured photos, scored condition and generated a report a client could open on their phone. Clients signed off faster. Fewer calls asking what was actually wrong with the car. Invoices went out the same day the work was approved.

CheckA+ is that same system, built for other workshops, dealers, fleets and insurers to run on their own brand. The checklist, the report and the client portal are the same ones we use every day — not a separate product built to look good in a demo.

We're a small team, and we build for the people actually running inspections on the floor — not the other way around.
